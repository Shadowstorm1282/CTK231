My History

My interest in game design started when I was a kid, playing games like Minecraft and Skylanders. I was fascinated by the creativity and freedom these games offered, and I wanted to learn how to create my own games. As I got older, I started exploring different games and the engines behind them, I was so facinated by them to the point where I decided to pursue a degree in game design.
